# Midnight Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Midnight is designed as a local-first daily discipline tracker. The app stores your quests, completion history, ranks, awards, archive, reminder preferences, theme preference, and backup data on your device.

## Data Stored On Device

Midnight stores app data locally using device storage. This can include:

- quest titles, categories, schedules, difficulty, completion status, and contract status
- completion history, Discipline Rating, ranks, streaks, and awards
- archived quests
- reminder preferences and local notification schedule identifiers
- theme preference
- backup JSON, including reminder preferences, that you generate or paste inside Settings

## Data Collection

The current app does not create an account, upload your data to a Midnight server, use analytics SDKs, or use advertising SDKs.

If you enable reminders, Midnight schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder content and timing are handled by the operating system and are not uploaded to a Midnight server.

Local coaching prompts are generated on your device from your saved quests and evaluation history.

If you choose to export a backup, the app generates a JSON backup on your device. You control where that backup is copied, stored, or shared.

## Data Sharing

Midnight does not sell personal data and does not share your app data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Data Deletion

You can delete app data by clearing the app's storage from your Android device settings, uninstalling the app, clearing archived quests in Settings, or using any in-app reset controls that are available in your build.

## Future Features

If cloud sync, AI coaching, accounts, payments, analytics, or support messaging are added later, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety answers must be updated before release.
